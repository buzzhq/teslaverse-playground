The idea in 30 seconds

Sum of the parts: we value each Tesla business on its own (cars, energy storage, robotaxi), add the cash Tesla holds minus its debt, then divide by the number of shares. Each business is valued with a DCF: we guess its cash flow over the next 10 years, add a "terminal value" for everything after that, and shrink future dollars back to today's dollars using a discount rate.

The starter settings reproduce the Teslaverse Valuation Model (v4). Every number is a guess you can change - that's the point. Tap i next to any slider for a plain-English explanation.
