Overview

Sum of the parts: we value each Tesla business on its own (cars, energy storage, robotaxi, Optimus robots, FSD software), add the cash Tesla holds minus its debt, then divide by the number of shares. Each business is valued with a DCF: guess its cash flow for 10 years, add a "terminal value" for everything after, and shrink future dollars back to today's using a discount rate.

Starter settings reproduce the Teslaverse Valuation Model v4 (cars, energy, robotaxi, cash = US$50.30) and add Optimus and FSD, which live only on this site. Tap Explain ▾ under any slider.

    Optimus

    • Production went from a few dozen a week (Q2 2026) to several hundred a week (Aug 2026); a >1,000/week line is targeted by end-2026 (The Information via Electrek).
    • No price, orders or release date published; first robots are used inside Tesla. Musk targets public sales in late 2027.
    • Rivals: Unitree shipped ~5,500 humanoids in 2025 (G1 from US$13,500); Agility Digit, Figure 03 (BMW), Boston Dynamics Atlas (Hyundai).
    • Model: price starts at US$60k (assumption), falling each year; every Optimus number is an assumption.

    FSD (Full Self-Driving)

    • 1.48m active paid FSD customers at 30 Jun 2026 (+56% in a year); ~55% bought upfront, ~45% subscribe.
    • Subscription-only since 14 Feb 2026: US$99/month. Approved in the US, Canada, Australia, NZ, parts of Europe; China launched May 2026.
    • Still "Supervised": the driver must watch the road. No licensing deal signed with any carmaker.
    • Avoiding double counting: today's FSD revenue is already inside car revenue (and the car margin), so only subscribers above today's ~0.67m monthly subscribers count here. Upfront purchases stay in Cars. Robotaxi fares are valued only in the Robotaxi section, and Optimus has no overlap.
